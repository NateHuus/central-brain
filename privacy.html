Central Brain privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-31. Central Brain is an internal tool run by Nate Huus (natehuus@gmail.com). It is used only by its operator's own Google account.

What Central Brain accesses

  • Google Drive: it searches for and reads files named run-report-* that teams have shared with the operator's account. It does not open, change or delete any other file.
  • Google Sheets: it reads and writes two spreadsheets owned by the operator, one for run counts and one for the list of teams.
  • Gmail: it sends alert emails from the operator's account to the operator's own address. It does not read the mailbox.

What the data is

Run reports hold counts, job names, hours saved and short status lines. They contain no client names, contact details or deal figures. The team names that teams register with us are the only identifying data.

Use, sharing and keeping

The data is used only to check that each team's system is running and to send the operator alerts. It is not sold, not shared with anyone else and not used for advertising. Records stay in the operator's spreadsheets while a team uses the system and are removed when the team stops and asks for removal.

Google user data

Central Brain's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: natehuus@gmail.com.

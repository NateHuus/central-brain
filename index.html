Central Brain

An internal operations tool run by Nate Huus.

Central Brain checks that the Team Brain systems we set up for real estate teams keep running. Each team's computer writes a short weekly run report: how many jobs ran, how many ran cleanly, and the hours saved. The team shares one Drive folder of those reports with us. Central Brain reads the reports, records the counts in a spreadsheet we own, and emails us if a team goes quiet or a backup is overdue.

Run reports hold counts and job names only. They never contain client names, contact details or deal figures.

Central Brain is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.
